<h2><a href="https://leetcode.com/problems/container-with-most-water/">11. Container With Most Water</a></h2><h3>Medium</h3>

<p>Our goal is to maximize area in this problem. The idea is that as our pointers keep shifting inwards (so the width keeps getting smaller), we want to choose the pointer where the height of the vertical line is the tallest amongst the two. Begin by initializing two pointers, left and right to their respective indices. Keep a res variable which will hold the max area of water we can contain that we have seen so far. While our left pointer is smaller than our right, calculate the area by taking the minimum height between the heights at left and our right pointer and multiply that by the width, so that will be right - left. Store the max between the area calculated and the value already at res. If the height at our left pointer is smaller than the height at our right, increment the left pointer. Otherwise, increment the right pointer. After the while loop exits, we should have found the maximum amount of water that our container can store. Return res.</p>
