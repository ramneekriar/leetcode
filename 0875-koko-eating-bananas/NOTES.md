<h2><a href="https://leetcode.com/problems/koko-eating-bananas/">875. Koko Eating Bananas</a></h2><h3>Medium</h3>

<p>Set the left pointer to be 1, and the right pointer to be the max bananas that exist in a pile. We are trying to find the min bananas to eat per hour (calculated by the midpoint) such that the hours it takes is equal or less than the time constraint given. Initialize a res var to be equal to right, as we have to take the highest number (ie. you cannot eat 2.5 per hour, the rate will have to be 3). While left is smaller or equal to right, calculate the midpoint. Set the hours to be 0, since we are calculating this number at every midpoint. For bananas in each pile, add the hours to be the ceiling division of bananas divided by the midpoint (curr rate). If after this our hours is less than the time constraint given, then calculate the min eating speed between res and the curr eating speed. Move our right pointer to (mid - 1) as we can explore a lower eating speed. If hours is bigger than the given time constraint, move left to be (mid + 1), as we need to increase our min eating speed. At the end, return the answer we have in res.</p>
