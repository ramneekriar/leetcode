<h2><a href="https://leetcode.com/problems/best-time-to-buy-and-sell-stock/description/">121. Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock</a></h2><h3>Easy</h3>

<p>Initialize a left pointer to 0 and right pointer to 1. Our right pointer is what is going to be looping through each and every index while our left pointer acts as our anchor point holding on to the best buying price. Initialize a res variable to 0. While right is less than the length of prices, compare and check that the price at our left pointer is smaller than the right. If so, calculate the profit and store the max profit between res and profit into res. Else, this is the case where both prices are equal or it results in a negative profit, we set left equal to our right pointer. Our right pointer increments by 1 for the while loop. In the end, return res which holds our max profit.</p>
